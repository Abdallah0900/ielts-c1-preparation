About This Project

This is a structured, evidence-based curriculum for IELTS preparation targeting Band 7–8. It uses CEFR C1 as a language-proficiency reference, while respecting the distinct nature of both frameworks.

All activities, examples, and practice materials are original. Band descriptors are summarized from publicly available official documentation. No copyrighted IELTS materials are reproduced.

Disclaimer: AI-generated assessment is for formative learning purposes only and is not an official IELTS score.
